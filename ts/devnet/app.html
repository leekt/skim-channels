Where the price comes from

From the feed to each fill

Latest batch
  1. 1
    Chainlink ETH/USD
    Apps read it with latestRoundData() on its proxy, which asks the current aggregator for its latest round.
  2. 2
    The L1 node
  3. 3
    The app executor calls the feed at its L1 address
    The proxy and aggregator (and the channel) are imported read-only: their real code runs against L1 storage at the head the executor follows, every value proven against that head's state root. The executor moves to each new head by itself. Nothing is copied or relayed.
  4. 4
    L1 checks the same price when the batch is proposed
    The session's ChainlinkAdapter verifies proofs of the slots that call read, at the block each oracle segment read it: the proxy's code and its aggregator are the pinned ones, the round is the latest at that block, and it was fresh at that block's time.
  5. 5
    Each fill is checked against that price
    The executor refuses a fill more than 1% from it, and anyone can prove such a fill on L1 with challengeFill.
