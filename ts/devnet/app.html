Where the price comes from

From Sepolia to each fill

Latest batch
  1. 1
    Sepolia · Chainlink ETH/USD
    The feed's aggregator keeps the latest answer in two storage slots: the round id, and that round's answer.
  2. 2
    skim node · Sepolia light client
    skim follows Sepolia's head and serves storage proofs at the safe block, which the beacon chain has justified.
  3. 3
    FeedMirror on the L1 devnet
    A contract verifies both proofs against the Sepolia header's state root and stores the price.
    Trusted: that this header is on Sepolia's canonical chain. The devnet can't check Sepolia block hashes, so only the relay may submit headers. Every step after this one is verified.
  4. 4
    The batch pins an L1 block and proves the price slot
    The executor reads FeedMirror only through a storage proof at one L1 block. L1 checks the same proof again when the batch is proposed.
  5. 5
    Each fill is checked against that price
    The executor refuses a fill more than 1% from the proven price, and anyone can prove such a fill on L1 with challengeFill.
